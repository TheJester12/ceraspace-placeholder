Ceraspace is Shutting Down

We are sharing some bittersweet news about Ceraspace. After much thought, we, Alex, Ty, and Connor, have made the difficult decision to shut down Ceraspace in the near future.

When we started Ceraspace, our goal was to build a tool that made managing ceramics studios easier and more enjoyable. Over the past year and a half, we’ve been incredibly grateful for your support, feedback, and creativity. However, as we assessed the future of Ceraspace, we realized we couldn’t deliver the level of innovation and long-term commitment that you and your studios deserve.

We’re proud to have been part of this community, and we want to ensure you have the best tools to continue running your studios seamlessly. That’s why we’re now recommending Kiln Fire. Kiln Fire is designed specifically for community-based pottery studios, offering powerful features to manage memberships, classes, and more. We believe it will be a great fit for many of you and recommend you schedule a demo with them.

Here’s what you need to know about the transition:‍

  • Ceraspace will officially shut down on April 30th, 2025.
  • Export your data: Please ensure you export any important information from your account before this date. Instructions on how to do this can be found here.
  • Questions or support: If you need help during this transition, feel free to reach out to us.

We know this news might be disruptive, and we deeply apologize for any inconvenience it may cause. Closing Ceraspace was not an easy decision, but we’re confident that Kiln Fire can help take your studios to the next chapter.

Thank you again for trusting us with your studios. It’s been a privilege to support your incredible work, and we can’t wait to hear how your studios grow in the future.

- Alex, Ty, and Connor
Founders of Ceraspace
